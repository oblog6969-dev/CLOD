# 🔺 Abraham Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs & Major Segments

> [!info] Framework Overview
> **Originator:** Dr. Abraham Maslow (*Motivation and Personality*, *Toward a Psychology of Being*)  
> **Structure:** 6 Tiers (Base Somatic to Apex Transcendence)  
> **Granularity:** 18 Major Segments (3 per tier)  
> **Key Dimensions:** Primary Center of Gravity, D-Need (Deficiency) vs. B-Need (Being/Growth) Orientation  
> **Implementation Files:** `traits.js`, `compatibility.js`, `script.js`, `style.css`

## 🏛️ The 6 Tiers & 18 Major Segments

| Tier | Category | Major Segments | Clinical Meaning |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **✨ Self-Transcendence (Apex)** | **B-Need** | 1. Transpersonal Mission & Legacy<br>2. Altruism & Generative Service<br>3. Spiritual Unity & Meaning | Dedication to causes larger than self, community contribution, and existential coherence. |
| **🌟 Self-Actualization** | **B-Need** | 1. Authenticity & Values Alignment<br>2. Creative Potential & Expansion<br>3. Personal Sovereignty & Freedom | Autonomous self-direction, intellectual/creative flourishing, and living by inner principles. |
| **🏆 Esteem, Mastery & Respect** | **D-Need** | 1. Self-Worth, Dignity & Sovereignty<br>2. Competence & Mastery<br>3. Mutual Admiration & Validation | Dignity, professional competence, social standing, and reciprocal validation. |
| **💖 Love, Belonging & Intimacy** | **D-Need** | 1. Deep Emotional Intimacy<br>2. Unconditional Acceptance<br>3. Companionship & Connection Rituals | Vulnerable emotional attunement, warm non-judgmental acceptance, and consistent companionship. |
| **🛡️ Safety, Security & Predictability** | **D-Need** | 1. Financial Predictability<br>2. Emotional Safety & Container<br>3. Domestic Order & Family Boundaries | Economic prudence, calm non-threatening communication, and domestic structure. |
| **🌿 Somatic Homeostasis & Rhythm** | **D-Need** | 1. Rest, Sleep & Recovery<br>2. Nervous System Grounding<br>3. Physical Vitality & Sensory Ease | Sleep protection, stress down-regulation, physical energy balance, and sensory ease. |

## ⚖️ Deficiency Needs (D-Needs) vs. Growth Needs (B-Needs)

- **D-Needs (Deficiency: Somatic, Safety, Belonging, Esteem)**:
  - Motivated by absence; essential for survival, regulation, and emotional safety.
  - Deficiency triggers anxiety, defensive reactivity, or territorial contraction.
- **B-Needs (Being/Growth: Self-Actualization, Self-Transcendence)**:
  - Motivated by expansion, creativity, and spiritual purpose.
  - Flourishes only when D-Need foundations are sufficiently stable.
- **Orientation Categories**:
  - *Deficiency & Security Anchored* ($D \ge 65\%$)
  - *Growth & Actualization Driven* ($B \ge 40\%$)
  - *Balanced Integrative* (Harmonious D/B equilibrium)

## 🔗 Dyadic Compatibility Dynamics

In relationships, differences in the primary center of gravity define dyadic dynamics:
- **Complementary Stability vs. Exploration**: When one partner anchors domestic safety while the other explores creative growth.
- **Risk of Stagnation vs. Drift**: Couples need both grounding (D-Needs) to prevent burnout and aspiration (B-Needs) to avoid relational stagnation.
