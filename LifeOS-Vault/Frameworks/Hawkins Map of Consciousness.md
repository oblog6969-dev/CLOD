## 🔗 Dyadic Compatibility Impact
When one partner operates below 200 (in Force, fear, or blame) and the other operates above 200 (in Power, acceptance, or reason), significant emotional drag occurs. MatchWise plots both partners on an interactive spectrum ladder with the 200 threshold clearly highlighted.
