# 🏔️ The Birkman Method (Iceberg Architecture)

> [!info] Framework Overview
> **Originator:** Dr. Roger W. Birkman  
> **Core Concept:** Human behavior exists on three distinct layers: surface behavior, subconscious needs, and reactive stress behavior.  
> **Visualizer:** Tri-Layer Submerged Iceberg Cross-Section (`renderBirkmanIceberg`)  
> **Implementation Files:** `traits.js`, `compatibility.js`, `script.js`

---

## 🌊 The 3 Iceberg Layers

```text
                  ▲
                 / \     [ Level 1: Usual Style ] (Observable Behavior)
             ───/───\─── Waterline ──────────────────────────────
               /     \
              /       \  [ Level 2: Underlying Needs ] (How one expects to be treated)
             /         \
            /___________\ [ Level 3: Stress Reaction ] (When needs are unmet)
```

1. **Level 1: Usual Style (Above the Waterline)**: Productive, effective, observable interpersonal habits.
2. **Level 2: Underlying Needs (Below the Waterline)**: Subconscious criteria required for emotional safety, comfort, and motivation.
3. **Level 3: Stress Reaction (Deep Submerged)**: Ineffective, defensive reactions triggered when underlying needs remain unmet over time.

---

## 🔗 Dyadic Misunderstandings
The most frequent interpersonal breakdowns occur when Partner A assumes Partner B needs what Partner A shows on the surface (Usual Style), rather than uncovering Partner B's distinct hidden Needs.
