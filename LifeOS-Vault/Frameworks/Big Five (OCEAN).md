# 🌊 The Big Five Personality Traits (OCEAN)

> [!info] Framework Overview
> **Originators:** Costa & McCrae, Goldberg  
> **Core Concept:** The gold standard of empirical psychometric personality research.  
> **Visualizer:** Comparative Horizontal Bar Chart with Divergence Deltas  
> **Implementation Files:** `traits.js`, `compatibility.js`, `script.js`

---

## 🧭 The 5 Dimensions

| Trait | High Scorers | Low Scorers | Dyadic Sensitivity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Openness (O)** | Curious, imaginative, intellectual, artistic | Conventional, concrete, routine-oriented | Shared interest in novelty vs. tradition |
| **Conscientiousness (C)** | Organized, disciplined, goal-driven | Spontaneous, flexible, relaxed | Household order, financial discipline |
| **Extraversion (E)** | Outgoing, energetic, socially assertive | Reserved, reflective, quiet | Social calendar and recharge needs |
| **Agreeableness (A)** | Empathetic, trusting, cooperative | Competitive, skeptical, direct | Empathy in arguments; negotiation ease |
| **Neuroticism (N)** | Emotionally reactive, prone to worry | Resilient, calm, emotionally stable | Vulnerability to relationship anxiety |
