# 🌈 Hicks 22-Step Emotional Guidance Scale

> [!info] Framework Overview
> **Originators:** Esther & Jerry Hicks (*Ask and It Is Given*)  
> **Core Concept:** A 22-level vibrational continuum mapping real-time emotional set-points from high vitality to despair.  
> **Visualizer:** Vibrational Gradient Continuum Bar  
> **Implementation Files:** `traits.js`, `compatibility.js`, `script.js`

---

```text
  [ High Vibrational Resonance ]

  4. Positive Expectation / Belief
  5. Optimism
  6. Hopefulness
  7. Contentment
  --------------------------------- [ Pivot Zone ]
  8. Boredom
  9. Pessimism
  10. Frustration / Irritation / Impatience
  11. Overwhelment
  12. Disappointment
  13. Doubt
  14. Worry
  15. Blame

  18. Revenge
  19. Hatred / Rage
  20. Jealousy
  21. Insecurity / Guilt / Unworthiness
  22. Fear / Grief / Depression / Despair / Powerlessness
  [ Low Vibrational / Contractive ]
```

## 🔗 Dyadic Emotional Agility
MatchWise calculates each individual's median set-point and emotional agility (speed of ascending the scale), highlighting when one partner's emotional state creates an anchor or a lift for the other.
