> [!info] Framework Overview
> **Originator:** Dr. Taylor Hartman (*The Color Code*)

---

## 🧭 The 4 Core Motives

```text
       🔴 RED (Power)
          /         \
         /           \
 🔵 BLUE (Intimacy) - 🟡 YELLOW (Fun)
         \           /
          \         /
       ⚪ WHITE (Peace)
```

| Color | Core Motive | Natural Strengths | Potential Blindspots |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🔴 Red** | **Power / Results** | Decisive, visionary, goal-driven, strong leadership | Impatient, blunt, reluctant to show vulnerability |
| **🔵 Blue** | **Intimacy / Connection** | Deep empathy, loyal, moral integrity, detail-oriented | Over-demanding of self/others, prone to worry, easily hurt |
| **⚪ White** | **Peace / Clarity** | Calm, diplomatic, patient listener, accepting | Conflict-avoidant, indecisive, passive resistance |
| **🟡 Yellow** | **Fun / Enthusiasm** | Spontaneous, charismatic, optimistic, expressive | Easily bored, neglects details, resists heavy commitments |

---

## 🔗 Dyadic Dynamics
- **Red + Blue:** High productivity and execution, but risk of emotional disconnect if Red's directness wounds Blue's sensitive nature.
- **Red + White:** Natural harmony (Red leads, White supports), provided Red avoids bulldozing White's boundaries.
- **Blue + White:** Gentle and safe, but may stagnate in unexpressed grievances.
- **Yellow + Any:** Injects humor, levity, and enthusiasm, balanced by structure.
