# 🎯 DISC Behavioral Assessment

> [!info] Framework Overview
> **Originator:** William Moulton Marston (*Emotions of Normal People*)  
> **Axes:** Pace (Fast/Active vs. Steady/Deliberate) & Focus (Task-Oriented vs. People-Oriented)  
> **Visualizer:** 2x2 Quadrant Cartesian Matrix with Tempo Bridge  
> **Implementation Files:** `traits.js`, `compatibility.js`, `script.js`

---

## 🧭 The 4 Quadrants

```text
                  Fast / Outgoing Pace
                           ▲
             [ D ]         │         [ I ]
           Dominance       │       Influence
      (Decisive, Direct)   │  (Optimistic, Engaging)
                           │
Task-Focused ──────────────┼────────────── People-Focused
                           │
             [ C ]         │         [ S ]
        Conscientiousness  │      Steadiness
      (Accurate, Quality)  │  (Calm, Empathetic)
                           ▼
                Steady / Reflective Pace
```

---

## 🔗 Dyadic Compatibility & Bridges
- **High D + High S:** Complementary (D drives forward momentum, S protects team cohesion and pace).
- **High I + High C:** Complementary (I generates excitement, C supplies analytical rigour and risk audits).
- **Tempo Bridge:** Measures divergence in decision speed (Fast vs. Deliberate).
