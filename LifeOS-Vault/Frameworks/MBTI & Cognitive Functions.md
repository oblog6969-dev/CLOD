# 🧩 MBTI & Jungian Cognitive Functions

> [!info] Framework Overview
> **Originators:** Carl Jung, Katharine Cook Briggs & Isabel Briggs Myers  
> **Core Concept:** 16 personality types derived from preferences across 4 fundamental psychological dichotomies.  
> **Visualizer:** Type Archetype Badges & Cognitive Function Matrix  
> **Implementation Files:** `traits.js`, `compatibility.js`, `script.js`

---

## 🧭 The 4 Dichotomies

1. **Energy Orientation:** **E**xtraversion vs. **I**ntroversion
2. **Information Processing:** **S**ensing vs. I**N**tuition
3. **Decision Making:** **T**hinking vs. **F**eeling
4. **Lifestyle / Outer World:** **J**udging vs. **P**erceiving

---

## 🔗 The 16 Types in MatchWise
- **Analysts:** INTJ, INTP, ENTJ, ENTP
- **Diplomats:** INFJ, INFP, ENFJ, ENFP
- **Sentinels:** ISTJ, ISFJ, ESTJ, ESFJ
- **Explorers:** ISTP, ISFP, ESTP, ESFP
