# 🏠 Gottman Sound Relationship House & The Four Horsemen

> [!info] Framework Overview
> **Originator:** Dr. John Gottman & Dr. Julie Schwartz Gottman  
> **Core Focus:** Marital stability, emotional safety buffer, and predictive divorce indicators.  
> **Visualizer:** Radial Emotional Safety Gauge & Four Horsemen Risk Threat Monitors  
> **Implementation Files:** `traits.js`, `compatibility.js`, `script.js`

---

## 🛡️ The Emotional Safety Buffer
MatchWise computes an **Emotional Safety Index (35% to 96%)** based on:
- Love Maps (knowing partner's inner world)
- Fondness and Admiration
- Turning Towards vs. Turning Away from emotional bids
- Positive Perspective buffer

## ⚔️ The Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse & Antidotes

| Horseman | Description | Toxic Impact | Antidote |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Criticism** | Attacking character rather than behavior (*"You always..."*) | Triggers defensive counter-attacks | **Gentle Start-up** (Use "I" statements) |
| **Defensiveness** | Playing the victim / counter-blaming (*"The problem isn't me, it's you"*) | Prevents mutual problem-solving | **Take Responsibility** for part of the issue |
| **Contempt** | Speaking from moral superiority, mockery, eye-rolling | #1 predictor of divorce | **Build Culture of Appreciation** |
| **Stonewalling** | Shutting down, physiological withdrawal, blank-staring | Blocks repair | **Physiological Self-Soothing** (20-min break) |
